## Featured projects

### [Aoun (عون): two-way LoRa distress and tracking system](https://github.com/Dalanazi/Aoun-SAIF-2026)

**Team Leader · idea owner and programmer · 2026**

A pair of devices that connects lost people to rescue teams beyond cellular coverage, with no internet, subscription, or satellite. One press sends an encrypted SOS with GPS location, and the rescue team's confirmation reaches the lost person so they know help is coming.

- **Built with:** LILYGO T-Beam (ESP32 + LoRa + GPS), AES-128-GCM encryption, sequence numbers for replay protection, and a registered-device whitelist
- **Results so far:** 10/10 security test cases passed, and 5/5 attack types rejected; encrypted SOS and confirmation verified on real boards
- **Recognition:** Qualified for SAIF 2026 · Saudi patent application filed (SAIP)
- **Status:** Proof of concept, with field range tests up next

### [MS SmartScan: AI-assisted MRI analysis](https://github.com/MS-SmartScan-Project/Healthon-MS-Project)

**Team Leader · 2026**

A decision-support tool that helps radiologists spot white matter lesions in MRI scans. It flags scans as suspected MS / SVD and shows the next clinical step, leaving the diagnosis to the doctor.

- **Built with:** Python, YOLO, OpenCV, a web scanning interface, and a separate patient registry
- **Recognition:** Accepted at Healthon 2026 (KSU Medical City) and pitched to the judging panel
